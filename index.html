About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Geophysics at Uppsala University, Sweden, supervised by Dr. Myrto Papadopoulou and Dr. Ayse Kaslilar Sisman.

My research interests include scientific machine learning for geophysics (AI4GEO), distributed acoustic sensing (DAS), surface-wave processing and inversion, neural operators, earthquake seismology, and full waveform inversion (FWI).

I received my M.Sc. in Geophysics from King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals and my B.Eng. in Exploration Technology and Engineering from Yangtze University.
