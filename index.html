About Me

I am a Ph.D. Student in Geophysics at Uppsala University, Sweden, supervised by Dr. Myrto Papadopoulou and Dr. Ayse Kaslilar Sisman.

I received my M.Sc. in Geophysics from King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM), supervised by Dr. Umair bin Waheed and Dr. Yangkang Chen, and my B.Eng. in Exploration Technology and Engineering from Yangtze University.

Selected Research

Bridging the gap between physics and DL for seismic.

Surface waves · Preprint, 2026

Physics-aware surface wave mode separation

A dispersion-guided deep inverse operator separates fundamental and higher modes without labeled training data.

PaperBibTeX
DAS · TGRS, 2025

Robust self-supervised DAS denoising

Attention-based deep image prior suppresses background noise in DAS-VSP data without clean training labels.

PaperCodeBibTeX
Reconstruction · Geophysics, 2024

Self-supervised 3D seismic denoising and reconstruction

Ground-truth-free learning with channel attention jointly suppresses noise and recovers missing seismic traces.

PaperBibTeX
