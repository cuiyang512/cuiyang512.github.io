# Homepage assets

`site.css` contains the static homepage's styles, including mobile layouts,
keyboard focus states, anchor offsets, and reduced-motion support.

Research images are optimized WebP copies of existing research figures. Clicking
a thumbnail opens the larger image. These are scientific results, not generated
illustrations.

- `surface-wave.webp`: synthetic mode-separation example from Yang Cui's
  surface-wave project (`figures/github/mode_sep_syn.png`), associated with
  [the 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12808).
- `das-denoising.webp`: the raw and proposed-method denoised panels (a and c)
  from Figure 8 in [Cui et al., TGRS (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2025.3533597).
  Panels are placed side by side with descriptive labels; data and colors are
  unchanged.
- `seismic-reconstruction.webp`: existing joint denoising/reconstruction figure
  (`source_ulca_reconstruction.png`) from the author's thesis assets, associated
  with [Cui et al., Geophysics (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1190/geo2023-0592.1).
